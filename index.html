Market Metrics Explained

How to read the data
GUIDE
Share Price

Latest valid closing price on the local exchange, shown in the security's trading currency.

Market Cap

Equity market value, broadly calculated as share price multiplied by shares outstanding.

P/E

Price-to-earnings ratio. It shows how much investors pay for one unit of earnings.

P/B

Price-to-book ratio. It compares market value with reported shareholders' equity.

YTD Return

Share-price change from the first valid closing price of the current calendar year.

Represents company revenue from the selected financial reporting period.
